Interpretable Machine Learning

For high-stakes decisions

Question. How do classification models balance accuracy and interpretability when decisions carry real consequences?

Approach. I evaluated Decision Tree, KNN, and Logistic Regression models on a breast cancer dataset, comparing performance metrics and interpretability tradeoffs.

Key insight. Logistic regression achieved the highest accuracy, while decision trees gave the clearest human-readable rules.

Why it matters. Model choice should reflect the decision context, not accuracy alone.

Nutrition Language Text Mining

NLP on scientific vs. informal writing

Question. Scientific papers and wellness blogs use the same nutrition words. Do they mean the same thing?

Approach. I built two corpora of nutrition writing, one from peer-reviewed research and one from blogs and popular media. I compared them with word frequencies, three sentiment methods, TF-IDF, LDA topic modeling, and keyword context windows.

Why it matters. No single NLP metric captured the difference. Health-related language needs contextual, domain-aware methods.

PythonNLPTF-IDFLDASentiment analysis

Most distinctive words in each corpus. Research papers lean on dietary, intake, risk, and metabolic. Blogs lean on food, eating, cravings, and cheat.
